Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sip ("the extension") is a Chrome extension for vocabulary learning. This policy explains what data is collected, where it is stored, and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Sip stores the following data locally in your browser using chrome.storage.sync:

  • Words you have saved (the word, its definition, phonetics, part of speech, example sentences)
  • Spaced repetition metadata (review intervals, ease factors, next review dates)
  • Your streak count and review history
  • App settings (AI provider URL, API key, reminder time, music volume)

This data is synced across your Chrome profiles via Chrome Sync if you have it enabled, and is never sent to our servers.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or telemetry
  • We do not collect your browsing history
  • We do not have servers that receive your word data or API keys
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

Third-Party Services

Sip may connect to the following third-party services at your explicit request:

  • Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) — used to fetch word definitions when you save a word. The word you looked up is sent to this public API. No personal information is included.
  • Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org) — used as a fallback for definitions. Same conditions as above.
  • AI Provider of your choice — if you configure an AI API (e.g., DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or a local model), your word data is sent to that provider to generate examples and brew stories. Your API key is stored locally in your browser and sent only to the provider you configured. We have no access to it.

Permissions Used

  • storage — to save your words and settings locally in Chrome
  • contextMenus — to add "Sip this word" to the right-click menu
  • notifications — to send daily review reminders (only if you enable them)
  • alarms — to schedule daily reminders
  • offscreen — to play ambient audio in the background
  • host permissions (https://*) — required so the extension can reach your configured AI API endpoint, which can be any HTTPS URL you choose

Children's Privacy

Sip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be noted in the extension's update notes.
